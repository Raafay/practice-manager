# Staff guide

Two pages to get you going. The app holds identity documents and tax references of real people, so take care. Never share your sign-in. Lock your screen when you step away.

## Signing in

1. The owner invites you by email. The link works once and lasts 72 hours.
2. Choose a password: at least 12 characters. A short sentence works well.
3. Set up two-factor. Scan the QR code with an authenticator app on your phone (Microsoft Authenticator, Google Authenticator or similar), then type the six-digit code. **Save the recovery codes** somewhere safe. They get you in if you lose your phone.
4. From then on, sign in at `/login/` with your email and password, then the code from your phone. On a computer only you use, you can choose to trust the browser for 30 days.
5. You are signed out after 60 minutes without activity, and after 12 hours in any case.

Locked out after five wrong passwords? Wait 15 minutes. Lost your phone? Use a recovery code, or ask the owner to reset your two-factor.

## Finding a client

- Type in **Search clients** at the top of any page, or press `/`. It finds clients by name, reference or company number, and people by name or email.
- **Clients** lists everyone. Use the filters (status, manager, service, tags) and save a filter you use often as a view.
- A client's page has tabs: **Info**, **Timeline** (everything that happened), **Contacts**, **Documents**, **Deadlines**, **Tasks** and more. Companies also have **Companies House**. Clients being onboarded also have **Onboarding**, **Risk** and **Engagement**.
- Tax references show masked (`••••••12A`). **Show** reveals one, and that is recorded.

## Your work: the three task views

All work, from compliance to advisory, is in **Tasks**. The **Overview** page shows your own work first: overdue, due today, this week, and anything waiting on a client.

- **Table:** a dense list. Filter, sort, tick several to change them at once, and save views.
- **Board:** columns by status (To do, In progress, Waiting on client, In review, Done). Drag a card to move it.
- **Calendar:** tasks by target date. Drag to plan; switch to deadlines to see what is due.

Open a task to see its steps. Tick them as you go: ticking the filing step marks the deadline done. Add comments, and type `@` and a name to tell a colleague. Press `n` anywhere for a new task, or use **New** at the top. **Time** has your timesheet and a timer.

## Logging a request

Clients' staff send requests through their request link, and those arrive under **Requests**. When a request comes by phone, email or WhatsApp, log it yourself:

1. **New**, then **Log a request**.
2. Choose the client, say who asked and how, and what they need.
3. On the request, choose **Make a task** (it suggests a due date three working days away), **Link to a task** that already exists, or **Reply by email**. A reply is queued like any other message.

## Approving queued messages

Nothing reaches a client until someone approves it. The exceptions are templates the owner has set to send by themselves.

1. **Queued** (the number shows how many are waiting).
2. Open a message. Check the address, the wording and the merge fields. Anything the app could not fill in is marked and stops the message from going.
3. **Approve and send**, or edit it first. Several can be ticked and approved together.
4. Watch for **Failed** and **Bounced: check the address**. Correct the contact's email and send again. Fee-chasing messages need a manager or the owner.

The banner "All automatic sending is paused" means nothing leaves until someone resumes it on the Queued page.

## Onboarding a client

1. A prospect who accepts a proposal becomes **Onboarding**. You can also add a client and set them to Onboarding. The **Onboarding** tab shows the checklist.
2. **Engagement** tab: prepare the engagement letter, check it, and queue it. The client signs on screen through their link.
3. **Risk** tab: record identity checks for the main contact, the directors and owners over 25%, with a copy of each document. These are sensitive, and each view is logged. Then fill in the risk assessment and submit it for approval.
4. Ask the client for their details through the portal (**Portal** invite on the Contacts tab), and track HMRC authorisations and professional clearance.
5. When every line is done, a manager or the owner presses **Make live**. Only the owner can go live with items still open, and must give a reason.

Questions or something looks wrong? Tell the owner. Never put real client details into an email to the app's developer or into any AI tool.

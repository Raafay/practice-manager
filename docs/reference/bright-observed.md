# What BrightManager looks like in use

Notes taken from photos of the practice's own BrightManager screens in October 2026. Use them to understand what staff are used to. Copy the structure where it helps. Do not copy wording, colours, logo or layout details exactly.

## 1. Top of every page

- Navigation: Overview, Tasks, Clients, Invoicing, Time, Resources, Tools, Queued (with a red count), Settings.
- A "Search Clients" box. Typing shows matching clients in a drop-down. Prospects carry a green "Prospect" badge beside the name.
- On the right: a help icon, a notifications bell, the user's initials in a circle, and the practice name.

## 2. Client page

Header

- Client name in large text. Beside it a red badge when the client has not registered on the portal.
- A row of buttons: Send Contact to Xero, Remove, New Time Log, Send Email, Send SMS, Generate Report.

Tabs, in this order: Client Info, Timeline, Tasks, Documents, LoE, Risk, Deadlines, Workflow, Invoices, Resources, Companies House, HMRC 64-8, Client Links, Contacts.

Client Info tab

- Left column: folding panels with a dark header bar and a chevron. Seen: Required Information, Company Details, Main Contact, Internal, Staff Tasks, Staff Task Monitor.
- Required Information holds: a wide "View on Companies House" button, Name, Client Type (a drop-down, for example Private Limited Company), a "Complete Credit Check" button, Partner, Manager.
- Main Contact holds: Person (a drop-down of contacts), Title, First Name, Middle Name, Last Name, a "Start AML Check" button, and a "Create Self Assessment Client" switch.
- Right column: a "Services Required" panel. One row per service with a switch and a pound box for the fee. Services seen: Accounts, Bookkeeping, CT600 Return, Payroll, Auto-Enrolment, VAT Returns, Management Accounts, Confirmation Statement, CIS, P11D, Fee Protection Service. An Update button sits at the bottom.

## 3. Sending a proposal

Opened from the client page as a pop-up titled "Send Proposal Email".

- To (the main contact), Bcc (the practice's own address), Subject (client name followed by "Proposal").
- Three switches: Show Breakdown Fees, Show Fees Inclusive of VAT, Show VAT Text.
- A rich text editor with the email. Its content, in order: a greeting, one sentence offering to act, a "Services Required" list of the services switched on, a "Monthly Charge" line with the fee plus VAT, a line inviting the client to ask for other services, a note about a one-off registration fee, then the steps to become a client.
- The steps the practice uses today: complete the online registration, and complete a Post Office identity checking form. Then a link to a short video about the client portal.
- Buttons: Send, attach a file, Cancel, and a signature drop-down that can be "No Signature".

## 4. Task list

Reached from Tasks. The address shows it is sorted by deadline.

- Columns: tick box, star, a red alert icon on some rows, Task name, Assignee (initials in a circle), Latest action (a drop-down, "No Latest Action" when empty), Deadline (a date in an amber badge), then three small buttons per row. The last is a tick to complete.
- Task names follow a pattern of work type plus period:
  - PAYE October 2026
  - Accounts Preparation Year End 31/01/2025
  - Companies House Submission Year End 31/01/2025
  - CT600 Submission
  - Confirmation Statement Period End 20/10/2026
  - Accounts Bookkeeping Period End 28/02/2026
- A confirmation statement with a period end of 20/10/2026 shows a deadline of 03/11/2026, which is 14 days later.
- The list contains only these compliance tasks. There is no board or calendar, and nowhere natural for advisory work. This is the main complaint.

## 5. Queued messages

Queued lists automatic emails waiting to go. Opening one shows a page titled "Email" with a "Details" panel:

- Send From: a staff member's name with the practice's address.
- Subject: for example a request to confirm details for a confirmation statement, with the company name.
- Email: a rich text editor. The confirmation statement email says it is automatic, gives the statement date and the date it becomes late, warns what happens if it is not filed, asks the client to reply confirming each section or saying "no change", then lists the data held: registered address and the sections that follow.

The practice edits and approves these before they go.

## 6. What to take from this

- Keep: the tab strip, folding panels, the services panel with switches and fees, the task naming pattern, sort by deadline as the default, the queue with edit before send.
- Improve: three task views, advisory work in the same pool, confirmations done on a portal form as well as by email reply, a real sales pipeline in place of a prospect flag, and a forecast.
